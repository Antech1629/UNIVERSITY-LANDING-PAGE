Admission Requirements

Join Antech University and take the first step towards a bright future. Our admission process is simple and straightforward.

Our 2025/2026 Intake is ongoing. APPLY HERE

Certificate Level Requirements

  • Minimum KCSE Grade of D-(minus) from a recognized secondary school in the country
  • OR: Have a certificate in Artisan for a respective course

Diploma Level Qualification

  • Must have a mean grade of C (plain) and above for a respective course
  • OR: Have a certificate from a recognised institution in the country for an Artisan Achievement

Degree Level Requirements

  • Have a KCSE mean grade of C+ (plus) and above
  • OR: A diploma from a recognised institution with fully working real-time projects

Master's Qualification

  • Must have a Degree from a recognised University in the country and a strong portfolio with real world projects
